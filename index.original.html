Provenance & Process

More Than an Image. A Story.

Elysian Resonance II emerged over three consecutive midnight sessions in the dead of winter. Elena began without preliminary sketches, letting raw earthen pigments and aged walnut oil dictate the cadence of each stroke.

The deep terracotta tones are formulated from authentic Tuscan red ochre mixed directly on the studio marble slab, producing an acoustic vibration of color impossible to replicate with commercial synthetic tubes.

Certificate of Authenticity

The Hand Behind The Work

Elena Vance

“I create work for people who seek stillness inside chaotic cities. My paintings are not decorative statements; they are physical traces of quiet moments, layered with mineral soils that have existed on this earth for millennia.”

Trained in traditional mural conservation in Florence and contemporary mixed media in Berlin, Elena founded Studio Monograph to bridge the historic integrity of museum publishing with the lived warmth of everyday spaces.
